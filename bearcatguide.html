Frequently Asked Sorting Questions

Containers don't need to be surgically sterile, but they should be completely empty. If a container held something messy or sticky, like laundry detergent, shampoo, or soup, give it a quick rinse to remove the residue and prevent a mess during the recycling process.

Paper cups are manufactured with a thin inner plastic or wax lining to keep liquids from soaking through the cardboard. Local paper pulping machines cannot separate this plastic film from raw paper fibers, making the cups non-recyclable.

Contamination causes major operational issues. Custodians have to manually pick out garbage, or the entire bin load must be thrown into the landfill trash. "Wish-cycling" costs the university extra landfill tipping fees.
